Class 9 Physics: Understanding Motion

Motion is the change in position of an object with respect to time. It is one of the basic concepts used to describe the physical world.

Distance and Displacement

Distance is the total length of the path travelled by an object. Displacement is the shortest straight-line change from the initial position to the final position, with direction.

Speed and Velocity

Speed tells us how much distance an object covers in a given time. Velocity tells us the rate of change of displacement and includes direction.

Speed = Distance / Time
SI unit of speed = metre per second (m/s)

Class 9 Chemistry: Atoms and Elements

An atom is the smallest unit of an element that retains its chemical identity. Atoms contain protons, neutrons and electrons.

Elements

An element is a pure substance made of only one type of atom. Examples include oxygen, hydrogen, iron and carbon.

Compounds

A compound is a substance formed when two or more different elements combine chemically in a fixed proportion. Water is an example of a compound made from hydrogen and oxygen.

Class 9 Mathematics: Algebra Basics

Algebra uses letters and symbols to represent numbers and relationships. It helps us solve problems when some values are unknown.

Example

Consider the equation:

x + 5 = 12
x = 12 - 5
x = 7

The value of x is 7 because adding 5 to 7 gives 12.

Class 10 Physics: Waves

A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy from one place to another. In a mechanical wave, particles of the medium vibrate around their equilibrium positions.

Types of Waves

  • Transverse waves
  • Longitudinal waves

Important Terms

Wavelength is the distance between two consecutive points in the same phase. Frequency is the number of complete vibrations per second.

Wave speed = Frequency × Wavelength
v = fλ

Class 10 Chemistry: Organic Chemistry

Organic chemistry is the branch of chemistry that mainly studies carbon compounds. These compounds are found in fuels, food, medicines and many everyday materials.

Hydrocarbons

Hydrocarbons are compounds containing only carbon and hydrogen atoms.

  • Alkanes contain single carbon-carbon bonds.
  • Alkenes contain at least one double bond.
  • Alkynes contain at least one triple bond.

Class 10 Mathematics: Quadratic Equations

A quadratic equation is an equation in which the highest power of the variable is two.

ax² + bx + c = 0
where a is not equal to zero.

Example

x² - 5x + 6 = 0

Factorizing gives:

(x - 2)(x - 3) = 0
x = 2 or x = 3

Useful Study Guides

How to Make an Effective Study Timetable

A study timetable helps students organize their time and balance different subjects. Start by listing your subjects and identifying the topics that need more attention.

  • Set a clear goal for each study session.
  • Divide large topics into smaller sections.
  • Take short breaks between study sessions.
  • Revise previously learned topics regularly.
  • Allow enough time for sleep and rest.

Why Understanding Is Better Than Memorizing

Understanding a concept helps students explain it in their own words and apply it to new problems. Memorizing alone may not help when a question is presented in a different way.

To improve understanding, read the explanation, identify its main idea, work through examples and try explaining the topic without looking at the textbook.

How to Prepare for Examinations

Good examination preparation begins with consistent learning rather than last-minute studying. Make a realistic plan and revise each subject regularly.

  • Understand the syllabus.
  • Practice questions from each topic.
  • Review mistakes and correct them.
  • Practice writing clear answers.
  • Keep your study materials organized.
